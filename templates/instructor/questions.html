Reported questions

Students can report a question from their review page. Nothing happens on its own: for each one, read it, then retire it (no student is given it again) or dismiss the report (it stays in use). You can also retire any question straight from a student’s review page. Retiring removes that exact question; if the same mistake shows up in many versions of a generated question, the generator itself needs fixing.
